How I work, in one example from my AI research

problem
AI models can produce different results depending on the dataset, evaluation method, and environment used to test them.
approach
Build reproducible evaluation workflows that collect structured results from both human reviewers and AI-based evaluators.
tools
Python, Google Colab, Hugging Face, structured datasets, and computing resources provided through the Digital Research Alliance of Canada.
focus
Automate data preparation and evaluation while keeping the results organized enough to analyze model behaviour, identify failure cases, and compare performance.
rule
Before trusting an AI result, understand how the data was prepared, how the evaluation was performed, and what the results actually measure.

My research work combines programming, data processing, and analytical thinking. I focus on making experiments reproducible, understanding why models behave the way they do, and turning evaluation results into useful conclusions.
